Standalone tool — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded. Entries are saved automatically in this browser, so you can close the tab and continue later on the same computer. Steps are worked through strictly in sequence within each section: select Initials 1 (performed) and Initials 2 (verified by a second person), fill in all entries and the remarks (“-” if none), then confirm Step completed → Yes. The step is then locked and the next step is released. Locked steps can only be reopened by the Head of Development (password); every reopening is logged. Calculated fields (dashed boxes) are evaluated automatically. Deviations must be documented in Notes & observations (Review tab) when they occur.
